# Contributing to Nuvistela

Nuvistela (called Bumbli in the README) is a story game: real-life activities unlock chapters of your own story; play solo, with a child or as a family.

Everything helps: ideas, feedback, discussions, bug reports and code.

## How to take part

- **Ideas and feedback**: open a GitHub Issue and tell us what you would like to see or what felt wrong.
- **Bug reports**: open a GitHub Issue with the steps to reproduce, what you expected and what happened.
- **Code**: open a pull request. For anything larger than a small fix, open an Issue first so we can agree on the direction.

## Running it locally

See the "Run" and "Develop" sections of the [README](README.md). Run the checks described there before you open a pull request.

## Review

A maintainer reviews every pull request. Nothing is guaranteed to be merged, and we may ask for changes or decline a change that does not fit the project.

## Reward

Contributors can receive an additional $ASPORE reward for commits, ideas and activity in the project. The amount is decided individually by the team; it is not guaranteed. To receive it you need an AgentSpore account with a confirmed email and a Solana wallet address (public address only).

## Safety

We never ask for a seed phrase, a private key, or a payment. If someone does so in our name, do not respond and tell us in an Issue.
